## How a roofing job runs, inspection to warranty

A roofing job is a sequence in which every step leaves you something to hold.

1. **Free inspection.** The roof and the attic, where reachable, get walked. Shingles or panels, flashing, valleys, vents and decking are checked, and the damage is documented. It is free with or without an insurance claim.
2. **Written estimate.** It arrives within 48 hours of the inspection, also free.
3. **Repair or replacement.** Leaks are traced from the stain to the entry point, since water travels along the deck. A residential roof covering permit applies once 25% or more of a roof inside the City of El Paso is replaced.
4. **Habits on site.** The crew phones if it runs late, tarps open roofs nightly and sweeps for nails with a magnet.
5. **Warranty paper.** Each installation carries a lifetime transferable workmanship warranty, printed beside the maker's warranty and handed over at the end. A leak that returns on this work gets fixed at no charge.
